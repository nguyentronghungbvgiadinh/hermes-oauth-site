Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About Hermes Local

Hermes Local is a personal AI assistant used by its owner to access Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

Hermes Local requests read-only access to Google Drive files for personal use.

Data Usage

Google Drive data is used to process the owner's requests through Hermes Local.

Data Storage

Files and extracted information may be stored locally for processing and retrieval.

Data Sharing

Hermes Local is not offered to other users. Google Drive data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data Deletion

The owner can delete locally stored data and revoke Google Drive access in Google Account security settings.
